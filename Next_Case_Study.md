#  Portfolio Update Plan & Case Study Workflow

## 1. How to Add the Next Case Study (3-Beat Framework)
To continuously maintain a high-impact portfolio, every new case study will follow this exact 3-step shape:

1. **The Problem:** Define the domain problem, business challenge, or target metric clearly.
2. **What I Did:** Outline the technical architecture, AI/ML models, stack, and methodology used.
3. **What Came Of It:** Showcase measurable outcomes, benchmark results (e.g., Silhouette score improvements), and key decision support.
